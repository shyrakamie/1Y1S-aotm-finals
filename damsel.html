ARTIST OF THE MONTH
NOVEMBER

Arthur Nery Now Playing: Isa Lang - Arthur Nery
Arthur Nery is a Filipino singer-songwriter from Cagayan de Oro, widely regarded as one of the defining voices of contemporary OPM R&B and neo-soul. He is celebrated for blending smooth R&B, neo-soul, indie pop, and soft alternative into an intimate, late-night sound driven by sparse guitar loops, warm basslines, and lush harmonies.
